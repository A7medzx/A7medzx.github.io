How an engagement works

Every test starts with written permission and a defined scope.

1. Scope

We agree on targets, dates, and rules in writing before any testing.

2. Recon

I map the application or network and its attack surface.

3. Test

Manual and tool-assisted testing, with every finding verified.

4. Report

Findings, evidence, severity, and fixes in one document.

5. Recheck

After you patch, I confirm the issues are actually closed.
